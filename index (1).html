📖 Foundations of Cheminformatics

Core principles of line notation, molecular descriptors, and three-dimensional chemical modeling.

What is Cheminformatics?

Cheminformatics integrates Chemistry, Computer Science, and Information Science to encode, store, index, and model chemical structures. By translating molecular graphs into machine-readable representations, researchers can rapidly predict physicochemical properties and screen large chemical libraries.

Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System (SMILES)

SMILES encodes a two-dimensional molecular graph as a compact ASCII string:

  • Atoms: Represented by atomic symbols (C, N, O, S). Lowercase letters (c, n) denote aromatic atoms.
  • Bonds: Single bonds are implicit; double (=) and triple (#) bonds are written explicitly.
  • Branches: Enclosed in parentheses, e.g., CC(=O)O for acetic acid.
  • Rings: Indicated by matching numeric ring-closure digits, e.g., c1ccccc1 for benzene.

⚖️ Physicochemical & Lipinski Metrics
  • Molecular Weight (MW): Average mass of the molecule in g/mol; influences diffusion and bioavailability.
  • LogP (Octanol–Water Partition Coefficient): Quantifies lipophilicity vs. hydrophilicity.
  • TPSA (Topological Polar Surface Area): Sum of surface contributions of polar atoms (primarily O and N, in Å²).
  • HBD & HBA: Counts of hydrogen-bond donors (OH, NH) and acceptors (O, N atoms).
🔗 Topological & Bonding Metrics
  • Sigma (σ) & Pi (π) Bonds: Single bonds contribute 1 σ bond; double bonds contribute 1 σ + 1 π; triple bonds contribute 1 σ + 2 π.
  • Rotatable Bonds: Single non-ring bonds attached to non-terminal heavy atoms; measures conformational flexibility.
  • Fraction Csp³: Ratio of sp³-hybridized carbons to total carbons; indicates 3D saturation vs. planarity.
  • Molar Refractivity: Reflects overall steric bulk and electronic polarizability.
🟢 Ball and Stick & 🟡 Stick Models

Ball and Stick renders atoms as scaled spheres connected by cylindrical rods, offering clear visibility of bond angles and hybridizations.

Stick Model emphasizes the covalent framework and connectivity without large atomic spheres.

🔵 Space Filling (CPK) & ⚫ Wireframe

Space Filling scales atomic spheres to their van der Waals radii, illustrating true steric volume and molecular surface crowding.

Wireframe provides a minimal skeletal line view ideal for inspecting complex cyclic cores.
